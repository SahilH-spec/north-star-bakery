Welcome to North Star Bakery

Fresh bread, handmade pastries, and celebration cakes, baked every morning for our neighbors. Whether you are grabbing a loaf on your commute, picking up treats for the family, or planning an event, we are glad you are here.

What We're Known For

  • Slow-fermented breads: crusty country loaves and our Signature Loaf, shaped and scored by hand each morning.
  • Handmade pastries and cookies: small-batch treats made with real butter and seasonal ingredients.
  • Celebration cakes: custom cakes and dessert trays for birthdays, family gatherings, and community events.

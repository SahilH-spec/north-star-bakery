The Story of North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery started as a small home kitchen with one oven and a big idea: a neighborhood should have a place where the bread is made by people you know. Our name comes from the star that has guided travelers home for centuries, and that is what we want our bakery to be for you.

Today we are still a small, family-run shop. We bake before sunrise so that commuters, families, and neighbors can start their day with something fresh and handmade.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread starts with good ingredients, and we keep our list short and honest.

  • Local first: we buy flour, eggs, and dairy from regional farms and mills whenever we can.
  • Simple ingredients: flour, water, salt, and time for our breads, with no artificial preservatives.
  • Seasonal baking: our pastries and cakes follow the harvest, so the fruit is always at its best.
  • Less waste: day-old bread becomes croutons, bread pudding, and donations to local community groups.
